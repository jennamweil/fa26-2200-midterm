Welcome to the BTU Lab!

The ATLAS BTU Lab is an inclusive community makerspace facilitating projects at the intersection of Art, Design, Engineering and Architecture. It serves multiple functions as a general workspace, and workshop host.

Everything You Need to Know

Who Can Join?

The BTU Lab welcomes makers of all experience levels. Our goal is to provide an inclusive environment where people can collaborate, experiment, and bring creative ideas to life.

New members must attend a 20-minute general orientation covering safety, equipment, and community expectations.

Check the calendar below for upcoming orientation sessions.

Guest access is subject to campus security restrictions.

Materials & Supplies

The BTU Lab is not a free supply store. Members are encouraged to contribute to the community in exchange for materials and resources.

Ways to contribute include volunteering, organizing supplies, displaying your work, sharing projects on social media, and offering workshops.

If you need something, we ask that you give something in return.
