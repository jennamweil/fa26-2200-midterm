"Intended Use of BTU Lab 3D Printers

  • Do not alter any part of the machine.
  • BTU uses PLA and ABS material, only. UNLESS other materials are agreed upon with BTU staff.
  • Do not train yourself on the use of the machine. There's more to it than YouTube thinks... We're here to help you learn, hands on.
  • Do not use third-party slicing software. Use Prusa Slicer, as specified for each machine.
  • ***We are updating our file management system to streamline your work. For now, please work directly with BTU staff to get your parts going.***

3D Printing Equipment & Materials

  • Prusa Mk4 FDM printer (x3)
  • Prusa Mk3S+ FDM printer (x3)
  • Prusa MINI2 FDM printer (x4)
  • Additional FDM printers available for exerimental projects. See staff!
  • 1.75mm PLA filament (black, and random colors)
  • 1.75mm ABS filament (black, and random colors)
  • Additional materials allowed only with staff approval.

TO GAIN FULL INDEPENDENT ACCESS TO LASER CUTTERS:

  1. Complete the laser cutter orientation. Calendar HERE.
  2. Class assignments, where you need assistance, are facilitated by LAs, TAs, and instructors first, then "Lazor Wizards", if available. (See below).
  3. As long as you need assistance, schedule laser appointments with a BTU Lazor Wizard listed below and on the BTU Mischief Makers page.
  4. Lazor Wizards provide "guided access". We will help you as much, or as little as needed.
  5. When you demonstrate independent and proper use of the laser cutter, you will have full access.
  6. Block 1-hour times on the whiteboard next to each laser. (Not during scheduled orientations.)
